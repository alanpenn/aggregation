# Extreme diagonals under strict convex incidence

This study uses the saved 40-line Hill maps with and without full
geometric metric reachability, and the saved 29-line angular-complete
and 40-line metric-complete Y maps. Each reference's exact selection,
all-pair angular calculation and fixed-connector geometric metric
calculation were checked against its saved record. The strict incidence
certificates and full-pool depths come from the same settlement lineage.

## Geometry and search

The six axes are the bands proposed in the earlier corridor study,
derived from its long-line clusters. To test whether a band has the
physical character of a corridor, the reassessment samples nine
cross-sections. On each side it measures the distance to occupied
space within 12 coordinate units. Seven or more two-sided samples
indicate a mostly two-sided band. Four to six indicate a mixture of
corridor and open space. Three or fewer give weak support for a
physical corridor. This is a bank-profile diagnostic on preset axes.
It does not claim to discover the settlement's street network.

Sheaf membership is a distinct geometric test on the full certified
candidate pool. A candidate must run through at least 28% of the axis,
remain within its recorded half-band and lie within 32° of the axis.
A diagonal must overlap 60%, have a measurable lateral displacement
and cross the approximate centreline. An opposed pair must intersect
exactly. The geometric ranking maximises total maximal-line length,
then the shorter diagonal's length, with rational endpoints resolving
ties. This ranking has no route inputs once the band is specified.

Each replacement holds the rest of its strict reference fixed. The
route-aware control exhausts the opposed-pair inventory for that band.
It evaluates every feasible pair on all eligible doorways and ranks
them by mean incidence-angular depth, then total length. The combined
control additionally requires every archived geometric connector
attachment to survive. Extreme-pair augmentation enumerates up to
three additions from ten shortlisted lines, preferring original sheaf
members, candidates covering missed regions and candidates restoring
external links or geometric attachments. A restore-original-plus-pair
fallback is tried when no shortlisted augmentation passes. Thus
the augmentation is bounded even though direct opposed-pair
enumeration is exhaustive within each geometric band.

Strict feasibility requires exact clearance, full interior traversal
coverage, nonempty doorway incidence, one axial component and full
winding rank. All eligible doorway pairs are checked through directed
axial segments with free entry and exit. Angular excess uses matched
pairs against the corresponding full pool. The records include the
worst doorway pairs and exact transverse contact IDs lost or gained.
A transverse contact is a contact with a retained exterior line whose
lateral displacement exceeds 0.65 times its longitudinal displacement
in the corridor frame.

## Geometric metric access

The fixed certified metric inventory is unchanged. Geometric
reachability does not veto the angular variants. For each unattached
metric doorway, the experiment independently tests an exact visible
segment to an interior point of a selected line in an incident convex
region. A separate audit reruns the nearest-point protocol on the
selected lines without the archived inventory cap. It distinguishes
doors that recover under an uncapped inventory from doors that need a
non-nearest attachment point. Those witnesses diagnose the protocol
while the reported metric depths continue to use the saved connectors.

## Hill 42

The first geometric band has no qualifying opposed pair. None of its
nine cross-sections finds both banks within the search radius. Bands 2
and 3 each show five two-sided sections, with median banked widths of
10.68 and 5.95, respectively. They represent mixed corridor and open
space on these axes. Each strict 40-line map retains only one line
in each band. An opposed-pair replacement therefore increases line
count from 40 to 41.

All four geometric replacements keep strict coverage, one component
and winding rank 11. The two band-3 geometric replacements both

complete metric reachability and seven new transverse contacts.

## What the metric failures mean

The audit examined 328 doorway-failure occurrences across valid angular
map variants, with repeats checked once for the same selected line set
and doorway. In 319 occurrences, an uncapped exact nearest-point search
found a valid connector. The other nine occurrences all concern Y
building 100. Its nearest-point search still failed, but the exact
interior witness supplied a valid straight connection. All failures
therefore have certified geometric access witnesses. The original
metric results continue to use the archived protocol and count its
unreachable pairs.

## Assessment

The geometric rule supplies a reproducible preferred pair once a band
is defined. Across the four references it found pairs for ten of
twelve bands tested. All ten passed the strict angular checks, so none
needed additions for missing regions, winding or angular reachability.
Two Hill tests had no qualifying geometric pair. Additional lines
in the combined tests addressed geometric metric attachment losses.

Route evaluation found a lower mean angular depth than the geometric
pair in all ten comparisons. The improvement varies from about
0.00005 in a Y band to 0.026. Some worst-pair differences are much
larger than the change in the mean. In particular, the longest
opposed pair is a useful low-cost first candidate, but it cannot
certify low angular regret or metric completeness from length alone.
The well-banked Y bands offer the strongest physical case for the
geometric rule. Even there the 29-line reference is already too
sparse to benefit in line count, and the 39-line combined improvement
comes from the route-ranked alternative.

The experiment changes one band at a time and evaluates the full
selected network for each replacement. It does not establish what
would happen if several bands were replaced together. The exhaustive
pair controls considered 606 reference-and-pair combinations across
these fixed bands. This is a check of the geometric heuristic in a
bounded setting, with no claim about global selection.
