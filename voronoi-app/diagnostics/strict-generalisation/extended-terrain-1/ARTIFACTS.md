# Extended corrected facewise terrain benchmark

The [report](report.md), [statistics](statistics.csv), [terrain summaries](terrain-summary.csv),
[paired differences](paired-terrain-differences.csv), and [vector axial atlas](numbered-axial-atlas.pdf)
cover the six terrain types across seeds 40–45. The [topology atlas](numbered-topology-atlas.pdf)
and [topology report](topology-report.md) document growth and accessible-space components.

The GitHub snapshot contains the vector maps, case journals, full-pool reference arrays, and visual
indexes. The local diagnostic archive additionally contains high-resolution PNGs, immutable
batch outputs with raw selected-map routing arrays, and source snapshots. Their original filenames
and checksums are retained in the export record. Restore the diagnostic archive to resume from
those raw checkpoints on another computer.

Hill and Y use the published corrected cohort at commit `def67112a4ec8cf2e1c8f0e4bd1f794edb512e45`.
